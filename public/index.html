Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CareerAI completely free?+
Yes! CareerAI is completely free. We use Groq's free tier for AI features. All core features including mock interviews, resume analysis, and career roadmap are available for free.
What file formats does the resume analyzer support?+
We support PDF, DOCX (Microsoft Word), and TXT files up to 5MB. The AI extracts text and analyzes it for ATS compatibility, keywords, and improvement suggestions.
How realistic are the AI mock interviews?+
Very realistic! Our AI uses Groq to generate role-specific questions based on real interview experiences. It adapts questions based on your answers, just like a real interviewer would.
How does the Career Roadmap work?+
You input your current skills and target role. Groq AI generates a personalized 6-month learning plan with specific topics, resources, milestones, and estimated timelines tailored to your profile.
Can I use CareerAI on mobile?+
Absolutely! CareerAI is fully responsive and optimized for all devices including Android and iOS. The mobile experience includes bottom navigation, swipeable cards, and touch-friendly interfaces.
What interview categories are available?+
We cover Frontend, Backend, Full Stack, React, Node.js, Java, Python, Data Science, and HR rounds across Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced difficulty levels.
